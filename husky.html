DOG BREED

Siberian Husky

The Siberian Husky is a working dog originally developed for pulling sleds. It is known for its beautiful coat, endurance and energetic personality.

Origin: Northeastern Siberia

Lifespan: 12–14 years

Weight: Approximately 16–27 kg

Coat: Thick double coat

Behavior & Personality

Huskies are friendly, social, energetic and independent. They are also known for being vocal and having a strong desire to explore.

Food Habits

Feed a complete and balanced dog food suited to their age, size and activity level. Measure portions and provide fresh water throughout the day.

Grooming & Care

Regular brushing is necessary to maintain their thick double coat. They shed heavily during seasonal coat changes.

Exercise & Activity

Huskies require substantial daily exercise. Walking, running and interactive games help keep them physically and mentally healthy.

Health Information

Potential concerns include hip dysplasia and eye conditions. Routine veterinary checkups and responsible breeding are important.

Living Conditions

Huskies need a secure environment and protection from overheating. In hot climates, exercise should take place during cooler hours.
