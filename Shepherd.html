DOG BREED

German Shepherd

The German Shepherd is an intelligent and versatile working dog. It is known for its loyalty, confidence and ability to learn different tasks.

Origin: Germany

Lifespan: 9–13 years

Weight: Approximately 22–40 kg

Coat: Dense double coat

Photo by gomagoti via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.5.

Behavior & Personality

German Shepherds are loyal, alert, intelligent and protective. They benefit from early socialization and consistent positive training.

Food Habits

Feed a complete and balanced diet suited to their life stage. Large-breed puppy food is useful during growth. Monitor portions to maintain a healthy weight.

Grooming & Care

Regular brushing helps manage shedding and maintain their coat. Their ears, teeth and nails should also be checked routinely.

Exercise & Activity

German Shepherds need daily physical exercise and mental challenges. Training, walking, scent games and interactive activities are excellent options.

Health Information

Potential concerns include hip and elbow dysplasia, degenerative myelopathy and bloat. Veterinary checkups and responsible breeding are important.
