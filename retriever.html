DOG BREED

Golden Retriever

The Golden Retriever is one of the most popular dog breeds. It is known for its friendly personality, intelligence and beautiful golden coat.

Origin: Scotland

Lifespan: 10–12 years

Weight: Approximately 25–34 kg

Coat: Dense double coat

Behavior & Personality

Golden Retrievers are generally friendly, affectionate, intelligent and eager to please. They often enjoy spending time with families and other animals.

Food Habits

Provide a balanced diet appropriate for their age and size. Portion control is important because Golden Retrievers can be prone to weight gain.

Grooming & Care

Brush their coat several times a week to remove loose hair and prevent tangles. Routine ear cleaning and dental care are also important.

Exercise & Activity

Daily walks, retrieving games and swimming can help keep them active. They also benefit from obedience training and mental stimulation.

Health Information

Potential health concerns include hip and elbow dysplasia, heart conditions and certain cancers. Regular veterinary checkups are recommended.
