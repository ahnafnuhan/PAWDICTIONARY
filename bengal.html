CAT BREED

Bengal

The Bengal is an athletic domestic cat famous for its beautiful spotted or marbled coat. It is energetic, curious and highly intelligent.

Origin: United States

Lifespan: 12–16 years

Weight: Approximately 3.5–7 kg

Coat: Short and sleek

Photo by Lightburst via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Behavior & Personality

Bengals are active, curious and playful. They enjoy climbing, exploring and interacting with their owners. They need regular mental stimulation.

Food Habits

Provide complete and balanced cat food suitable for their age and activity level. Fresh water should always be available.

Grooming & Care

Their short coat is relatively easy to maintain. Weekly brushing and routine nail, ear and dental care are usually sufficient.

Exercise & Activity

Bengals need plenty of exercise. Climbing trees, interactive toys and puzzle feeders help keep them physically and mentally active.

Health Information

Some Bengal cats may be predisposed to hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and inherited conditions. Regular veterinary checkups are important.
