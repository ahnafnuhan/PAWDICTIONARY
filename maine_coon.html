CAT BREED

Maine Coon

The Maine Coon is one of the largest domesticated cat breeds. Known for its long, thick coat, tufted ears and friendly personality, it is often called the gentle giant of the cat world.

Origin: United States

Lifespan: 12–15 years

Weight: Approximately 4–8 kg

Coat: Long and dense

Behavior & Personality

Maine Coons are generally friendly, intelligent, playful and sociable. They enjoy spending time with their owners but are not always overly demanding of attention.

Food Habits

They need a complete and balanced diet suitable for their age and activity level. High-quality cat food, fresh water and controlled portions help maintain a healthy weight.

Grooming & Care

Their long coat needs regular brushing several times a week to prevent knots and tangles. Regular nail trimming and dental care are also important.

Exercise & Activity

Maine Coons enjoy climbing, interactive toys and daily play sessions. Cat trees and scratching posts are excellent additions to their environment.

Health Information

Potential health concerns include hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, hip dysplasia and spinal muscular atrophy. Regular veterinary checkups are recommended.
