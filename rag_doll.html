CAT BREED

Ragdoll

The Ragdoll is a beautiful, large cat breed known for its blue eyes, soft coat and gentle temperament. It is often affectionate and enjoys human companionship.

Origin: United States

Lifespan: 12–17 years

Weight: Approximately 4–9 kg

Coat: Semi-long and silky

Photo by Simone Johnsson via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.0.

Behavior & Personality

Ragdolls are usually calm, friendly and affectionate. They often enjoy staying close to their owners and playing with interactive toys.

Food Habits

They require balanced cat food containing essential nutrients. Portion control is important to prevent obesity, especially for indoor cats.

Grooming & Care

Brush their coat two or three times a week to prevent tangles. Regular dental care, nail trimming and ear checks are also important.

Exercise & Activity

Ragdolls enjoy moderate daily activity. Provide toys, climbing spaces and short interactive play sessions.

Health Information

Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is an important inherited health concern in this breed. Routine veterinary checkups and responsible breeding practices are important.
